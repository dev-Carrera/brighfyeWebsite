What Is Brighfye?

Three ideas, fused into one map-first experience.

Brighfye is an exploration-based social media game — not just another feed, but a reason to leave the house.

Exploration

A map view where you see nearby posts, discover what places are hot, and queue up to explore a new spot with a random match based on distance or location preferences.

Social Media

Your feed comes alive on the FYP & Near Me views — in which you see a striking scene in Tokyo or a niche ice-cream shop just around the corner.

Videogame

"Join the Queue" pairs you with someone to go explore together, customize your profile as you travel, and fill an "I Explored" tab like a trophy cabinet.
